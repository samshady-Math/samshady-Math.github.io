PBMC’s New AI in Math Initiative

September 25, 2026 · 9:15 p.m. Eastern

I’m excited to share the University of Michigan’s Proof Based Math Club’s new initiative: AI in Math! We’ll be exploring how AI can support the development and understanding of mathematical proofs, while giving members hands-on experience with Lean and proof formalization. Our goal is to learn together, experiment with AI-assisted proof ideas, and investigate both the possibilities and limitations of these tools.
