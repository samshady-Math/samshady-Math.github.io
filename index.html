01 / Research

Geometry, explored
through computation.

The boundary of AdS³

My current research explores the boundary of three-dimensional anti-de Sitter space (AdS³) and the properties of spheres in this setting. I use SageMath and Mathematica to visualize and investigate the geometry through its representation in four-dimensional space.

I’m one of four students working with Neza Zager Korenjak through the University of Michigan’s Lab of Geometry, LOG(M).
